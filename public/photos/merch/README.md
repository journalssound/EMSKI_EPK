# Merch product photography

Drop product shots here and point `MERCH_ITEMS[].views` in
`src/components/website-draft/siteContent.js` at them.

## Naming

    blurred-faces-front.jpg
    blurred-faces-back.jpg
    blurred-faces-detail.jpg   (optional — sleeve E mark, print texture)

## Specs

- Square or 4:5 crop, shortest edge >= 1600px
- Dark or concrete backdrop (the site is near-black; white-cyc e-commerce
  shots read as print-on-demand and break the page)
- One hard light source, deep shadows — reference cobrahcore.com
- Keep the print unclipped and in focus; the cyan should stay saturated

## Source of truth

Industry Print Shop proof, design #19123 "Blurred Faces":
front 16" x 16.5", back 11.57" x 17", sleeve E mark 2.5" x 1.4",
black garment, White UL / White TL / TBD Blue inks.
